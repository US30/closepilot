# Acme Components Pvt Ltd: Delegation of Authority and Internal Controls

Synthetic document written for the ClosePilot demo. Thresholds match the agent code.

## Approvals

### DOA-1 Approval limits
An accounts payable executive may approve a vendor invoice below Rs 5,00,000 (5 lakh). An invoice of Rs 5,00,000 or more needs the approval of the Chief Financial Officer.

### DOA-2 Splitting of invoices
A purchase must not be divided into several invoices to keep each one under an approval limit. Two or more invoices from one vendor on the same date, each below the limit but together above it, are treated as a single transaction and need the higher approval.

### DOA-3 Segregation of duties
The person who creates or edits a vendor record must not approve payments to that vendor. Vendor creation, invoice booking and payment approval are held by different employees.

### DOA-4 Postings outside working days
Invoices are booked on working days. An entry posted on a Saturday or Sunday needs a written reason and is reviewed by the Financial Controller in the next close.

### DOA-5 Round-sum invoices
An invoice whose taxable value is an exact multiple of Rs 10,000 is supported by a rate card, contract or quantity breakdown before approval, because round-sum billing is a common sign of an estimated or fictitious charge.

## Vendor master

### VM-1 Vendor onboarding
A new vendor is created only after the PAN, GSTIN certificate and a cancelled cheque are collected and checked. The vendor master records who created the vendor and the date.

### VM-2 Change of vendor bank account
A request to change a vendor's bank account is confirmed by calling the vendor on the phone number already held in the vendor master, never on a number given in the request. Payments go only to the account recorded in the vendor master. A payment sent to any other account is stopped or recalled and reported as suspected payment fraud.
