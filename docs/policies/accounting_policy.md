# Acme Components Pvt Ltd: Finance and Accounts Policy Manual

Synthetic document written for the ClosePilot demo. Thresholds match the agent code.

## Bank reconciliation

### BR-1 Frequency and ownership
Every bank account is reconciled to the general ledger at each month-end close. The Financial Controller owns the reconciliation and signs it off within five working days of the period end.

### BR-2 Matching rules
A bank statement line is matched to a ledger entry when the payment reference and the amount agree. Where the reference is missing, a line may be matched on equal amount if the two dates are no more than 5 days apart. Any match made without a reference must be visible to the reviewer.

### BR-3 Bulk and consolidated payments
One bank debit may settle several ledger payments when the bank processes a payment batch as a single transfer. The reconciliation must list every ledger line that makes up the transfer and the sum must equal the bank debit exactly.

### BR-4 Bank charges
Fees and service charges deducted by the bank, with the GST on them, are recorded by journal entry in the month they appear on the statement: debit Bank charges, credit Bank. They are not left as reconciling items.

### BR-5 Interest credited by the bank
Interest earned on deposits and current account balances is recognised as Interest income in the month the bank credits it: debit Bank, credit Interest income.

### BR-6 Unidentified money received
A credit on the statement with no matching ledger entry is posted to the Unidentified Receipts suspense account. The treasury team must find the counterparty, normally a customer paying without a remittance advice, and clear the suspense balance within 7 working days.

### BR-7 Debits with no ledger entry
Money that left the bank account without a recorded payment voucher is treated as a possible unauthorised transaction. It is reported to the Financial Controller on the same day and raised with the bank. It is booked only after the payee and the supporting voucher are identified.

### BR-8 Outstanding and stale items
Ledger payments and receipts that have not yet cleared the bank are timing differences and need no action for the first 30 days. Items older than 30 days are followed up with the payee or the bank. A cheque not presented within three months is stale and is written back to the vendor account.

## Accounts payable

### AP-1 Purchase order required
Every vendor invoice must quote a valid purchase order. An invoice received without a purchase order is put on hold and returned to the requesting department for retrospective approval before it can be booked for payment.

### AP-2 Three-way match before payment
An invoice is released for payment only when it agrees with the purchase order and with a goods receipt note showing that the goods or services were received. No payment is made against an invoice that has no goods receipt note.

### AP-3 Price variance tolerance
The billed amount may differ from the purchase order value by at most 2%. A larger variance needs a revised purchase order or a written price approval from the Procurement Head. If the invoice is already paid, the excess is recovered by debit note.

### AP-4 Short receipts
Where the quantity received is less than the quantity ordered, the company pays only for the quantity received. The balance is paid after the remaining goods arrive, or the vendor issues a credit note.

### AP-5 Duplicate invoices
Before booking, an invoice is checked against invoices from the same vendor with the same amount dated within 10 days, including invoice numbers that differ only by spacing, prefix or a trailing character. A suspected duplicate is held. A duplicate that was already paid is recovered from the vendor or adjusted against the next payment.

## Taxes

### TAX-1 GST on vendor invoices
The tax charged must equal the taxable value multiplied by the applicable GST rate. Intra-state supplies carry CGST and SGST in equal halves and inter-state supplies carry IGST. A difference of more than 1 rupee from the computed tax is returned to the vendor for a corrected invoice, because input tax credit cannot be claimed on an incorrect invoice.

### TAX-2 Tax deducted at source
TDS is deducted on the taxable value at the rate held in the vendor master at the time the invoice is booked, and is deposited by the 7th of the following month. The vendor is paid the invoice total less TDS.

### TAX-3 GSTIN and input tax credit
Input tax credit is claimed only when the vendor's GSTIN on the invoice matches the vendor master and the invoice appears in the GSTR-2B statement for the period.
