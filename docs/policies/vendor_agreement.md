# Standard Supply Agreement between Acme Components Pvt Ltd and the Supplier

Synthetic document written for the ClosePilot demo. This is the standard contract every vendor signs.

## Commercial terms

### MSA-1 Prices
Prices are those stated in the purchase order and are fixed for the order. The Supplier shall not bill a higher rate unless the Buyer has issued a written amendment to the purchase order before dispatch.

### MSA-2 Delivery and acceptance
Goods are accepted when the Buyer's stores issue a goods receipt note. Risk and title pass to the Buyer on acceptance. The Buyer may reject goods that are damaged or not to specification within 15 days of delivery.

### MSA-3 Invoicing requirements
Each invoice shall carry a unique invoice number, the purchase order number, the Supplier's GSTIN and the HSN code. The Supplier shall not submit the same invoice twice. The Buyer may return any invoice that does not meet these requirements.

### MSA-4 Payment terms
The Buyer pays undisputed invoices within the credit period stated in the purchase order, between 30 and 60 days from the invoice date, by electronic transfer. No interest is due on amounts withheld because of a dispute raised in good faith.

### MSA-5 Supplier bank details
The Supplier's bank account is the one given at onboarding. A change takes effect only after the Supplier sends a request on its letterhead signed by an authorised signatory and the Buyer confirms it in writing.

### MSA-6 Taxes
Prices exclude GST, which the Supplier charges at the applicable rate. The Buyer deducts tax at source where the law requires and issues a TDS certificate each quarter.

### MSA-7 Shortfall and late delivery
If the Supplier delivers less than the ordered quantity, the Buyer may accept the part delivery and cancel the balance without liability. For late delivery the Buyer may deduct liquidated damages of 0.5% of the order value per week, capped at 5%.
